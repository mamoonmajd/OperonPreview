# OPERON Direction 4.5 — Source of Truth

The included `brand-reference/OPERON-Direction-4.5-Approved.png` is the approved visual identity and should be treated as the permanent source of truth unless the owner explicitly approves a redesign.

## Core philosophy
Business → Operations → Finance → Technology → Artificial Intelligence → Better Decisions

- AI is never the strategy. AI is the accelerator.
- Finance is the operating system that helps businesses make better decisions.
- Build capability. Not dependency.

## Four principles
- UNDERSTAND — Begin with the business.
- CONNECT — Align people, processes, systems, and data.
- SIMPLIFY — Reduce complexity.
- EMPOWER — Build capability and responsibly enable AI.

## Visual guardrails
Preserve warm limestone, Jordanian red, olive, bronze, charcoal, paper texture, editorial layouts, notebook references, subtle Tatreez geometry, architectural balance, and generous whitespace.

Do not introduce blue AI graphics, hexagon backgrounds, corporate stock photography, handshake imagery, laptop-pointing teams, fake holograms, luxury black-and-gold styling, or generic consulting layouts.
